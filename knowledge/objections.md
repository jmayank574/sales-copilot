# Objections playbook

## When to Use
Loaded into the brief prompt. The agent picks the one or two objections this lead would plausibly raise, and adapts the reply to the lead's business.

## Framework
Method: **LACE** = Listen, Acknowledge, Clarify, Educate. Ask ONE clarifying question before offering any proof. Replies are spoken-style and short. Proof points may only come from the Fernstone context file.

Tags: **[site]** = grounded in fernstone.com. **[assumption]** = generic broker objection, to be confirmed against what Fernstone reps actually hear.

## 1. "I already have a broker" [assumption]
- Type: competition.
- Clarify: "What would make you want to change anything, or is it all working for you?"
- Educate (site-grounded): Fernstone has a dedicated team that learns the business, and a platform for COIs, claims, policies and renewal alerts. Offer a second look, not a switch.

## 2. "I just need a quote fast / the cheapest" [site]
- Type: budget/urgency. The site says Fernstone is not a fit for people who want the fastest quote regardless of price, coverage or accuracy.
- Clarify: "What's driving the timeline: a contract, a renewal, something else?"
- Educate: The best price and coverage come from underwriters understanding the business, so a few details up front save time later. Fernstone keeps them updated on where the quote stands.

## 3. "I don't want to share all that information" [site]
- Type: information gap. The site says underwriters need operations, payroll, revenue, vehicles, locations and claims history.
- Clarify: "Which part feels like too much, or is it more about who sees it?"
- Educate: Underwriters can only price what they can see, and accurate information avoids surprises later. Fernstone says what is needed and why.

## 4. "My policy doesn't renew for months" [assumption]
- Type: timing.
- Clarify: "When does it renew, roughly? And is anything changing in the business before then?"
- Educate: Starting early leaves time to gather loss runs and compare options, and it avoids a last-minute scramble. No pressure to move before the renewal.

## 5. "How do I get my loss runs?" [site]
- Type: information gap. The site has a loss-run FAQ and says Fernstone helps track down reports.
- Clarify: "Which carriers have you had in the last few years?"
- Educate: Fernstone can help request them and make sure the history is presented accurately.
